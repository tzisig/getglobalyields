A single share of some US stocks costs more than many international investors' entire monthly investing budget. Fractional shares solve this - they let you buy a specific dollar amount of a stock or ETF rather than a whole share, which matters more for an international investor building a position gradually than it does for someone depositing a large lump sum at once.

---

**Interactive Brokers** offers fractional share trading across a large universe of eligible US, Canadian, and European stocks and ETFs, with a minimum fractional trade size as small as a few cents' worth of stock. This is available broadly across IBKR's international account base, not restricted to US residents - making it the most complete fractional-share option for the international investors this site is written for. ## Why This Matters More for International Investors Specifically

A US-based investor depositing $500/month into a brokerage account and a non-US investor doing the same thing face a subtly different problem: the non-US investor is often also converting from a local currency, meaning smaller, more frequent contributions are common - and whole-share-only investing forces either large idle cash balances waiting to afford a full share, or concentration in whichever stocks happen to be cheap enough to buy outright. Fractional shares remove that constraint entirely, letting every contribution go to work immediately across a properly diversified allocation.

---

## What to Check Before Relying on Fractional Shares

- **Which specific securities support fractional trading** - it is rarely every single listed stock or ETF; confirm your target holdings (including TQQQ or QQQ specifically, if that is part of your strategy) are on the eligible list.
- **Whether fractional shares carry full shareholder rights** - voting rights and certain corporate action participation can work differently for fractional positions depending on the broker; this rarely matters for a passive buy-and-hold investor but is worth understanding.
- **How dividends are handled on fractional positions** - typically prorated automatically, but confirm this operates as expected before assuming it.

---

## Bottom Line

For an international investor building a position gradually rather than depositing a large lump sum, fractional share access at Interactive Brokers, eToro, or XTB removes one of the more frustrating practical barriers to genuine diversification - being stuck choosing between concentration in cheap stocks or letting cash sit idle waiting to afford a full share of something more expensive.
