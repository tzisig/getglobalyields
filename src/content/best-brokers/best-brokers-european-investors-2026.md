Choosing a broker as a European investor comes with one constraint that does not apply almost anywhere else covered on this site: the PRIIPs regulation blocks direct access to US-domiciled ETFs like VOO and QQQ, regardless of which broker you use. The real question for a European investor is not "which broker gives me the widest access" - it is which broker offers the best UCITS ETF selection, the lowest costs, and correctly handles treaty documentation for the individual US stocks you can still buy directly.

---

## What Every European Investor Needs to Know First

Regardless of broker, the PRIIPs regulation means you will be buying Irish-domiciled UCITS ETFs (like CSPX for the S&P 500 or VWCE for global equity) instead of their US-domiciled equivalents. This is covered in detail in our country-specific guides for [Germany](/us-investing/how-to-invest-in-us-stocks-from-germany/), [Netherlands](/us-investing/how-to-invest-in-us-stocks-from-netherlands/), [Spain](/us-investing/how-to-invest-in-us-stocks-from-spain/), [Italy](/us-investing/how-to-invest-in-us-stocks-from-italy/), and [Poland](/us-investing/how-to-invest-in-us-stocks-from-poland/). Individual US stocks remain fully accessible - the restriction is specifically on packaged products.

## The Top Brokers for European Investors

### 1. Interactive Brokers - Best for Larger Portfolios and Full Market Access

[Interactive Brokers](/best-brokers/interactive-brokers-review-2026-the-best-broker-for-international-investors/) offers the broadest combination of UCITS ETF access, individual US stock access, and low costs of any broker serving Europe. Its treaty documentation handling through W-8BEN is integrated end-to-end, and its FX conversion rates are close to interbank - a meaningful saving for anyone regularly converting EUR, GBP, or other European currencies to USD.

**Best for:** investors with larger portfolios who want the full range of products (UCITS ETFs, individual US stocks, options, bonds) in one account.

### 2. DEGIRO - Best for Low-Cost ETF Investing

DEGIRO, headquartered in Amsterdam, is one of the most widely used brokers among European retail investors specifically for ETF investing, with a low-cost structure and straightforward setup. It is a strong choice for an investor whose portfolio is primarily UCITS ETFs with occasional individual stock purchases.

**Best for:** cost-conscious European investors building a UCITS ETF-based portfolio.

### 3. XTB - Best for Zero-Commission Trading Up to a Monthly Threshold

[XTB](/best-brokers/xtb-review-2026/) offers commission-free trading on real stocks and ETFs up to €100,000 in monthly trading volume, automatic interest on uninvested cash balances, and CNMV/multi-regulator coverage across several European markets. It is regulated locally in a number of European countries rather than only through an EU passporting arrangement, which matters to some investors for peace of mind.

**Best for:** investors trading below the monthly commission-free threshold who want a genuinely zero-cost entry point.

### 4. Saxo Bank - Best for Institutional-Grade Tools

[Saxo Bank](/best-brokers/saxo-bank-review-2026/) is a Danish investment bank offering a significantly broader product range and stronger research tools than the low-cost platforms, at a correspondingly higher cost structure. Fees decrease meaningfully at higher account tiers (Platinum at $200k+, VIP at $1M+).

**Best for:** investors who want more than execution - deep research, broad instrument access, and a bank-grade platform.

### 5. eToro - Best for a Simple, App-First Entry Point

[eToro](/best-brokers/etoro-review-2026/) is regulated across multiple European jurisdictions and offers a simpler, more visual interface than the platforms above, with copy-trading features that some investors find genuinely useful as a starting point. Its product range is narrower than IBKR or Saxo.

**Best for:** first-time investors who want the simplest possible onboarding.

## What to Check Before Committing

- **W-8BEN handling:** confirm the broker processes W-8BEN digitally and applies your country's treaty rate automatically to individual US stock dividends - this is standard at all five brokers above but worth confirming during onboarding.
- **UCITS ETF selection:** confirm the specific funds you want (CSPX, VWCE, or others) are actually tradable on the platform - selection varies slightly by broker.
- **FX conversion costs:** this is often a larger real-world cost than the headline commission rate, particularly for brokers that build in a spread rather than charging a transparent conversion fee.

## Bottom Line

For most European investors with a meaningful portfolio size, Interactive Brokers offers the best combination of cost, product range, and treaty documentation handling. DEGIRO and XTB are strong lower-cost alternatives for a primarily ETF-based approach, Saxo Bank suits investors who want more research and tools, and eToro remains the simplest starting point for a first account.
