Search "best robo-advisor" and you will get the same handful of names every time: Betterment, Wealthfront, Schwab Intelligent Portfolios. Nearly all of that content is written for US residents, and for good reason - these platforms are, in practice, essentially closed to non-US residents due to FATCA-related compliance restrictions that most US-focused robo-advisors have adopted. If you are researching this topic from outside the US, most of what you find will not actually apply to you.

---

## Why the Big Names Don't Work for You

Betterment, Wealthfront, and Schwab Intelligent Portfolios are built around US retail investors and generally require a US residential address and US tax identification to open an account. This is a compliance and business-model choice rather than a technical limitation - automated portfolio management involves ongoing regulatory obligations that these platforms have chosen to manage only for US-resident clients. Confirm current eligibility directly if you want to test this for your specific situation, but treat it as the default expectation rather than something worth spending significant time pursuing.

---

## What Actually Serves International Investors: Interactive Advisors

**Interactive Advisors**, the automated portfolio platform affiliated with Interactive Brokers, is the genuine option in this space for non-US residents. Because it operates on IBKR's existing international account infrastructure - the same infrastructure that accepts clients from 200+ countries for standard brokerage accounts - it extends meaningfully broader international access than the mainstream US robo-advisor names.

**What it offers:**
- A choice between traditional automated Asset Allocation portfolios (automatically rebalanced) and a large selection of thematic and manager-driven portfolios you can follow
- A management fee around 0.20% - competitive with, or lower than, most mainstream robo-advisors
- Automated rebalancing, with tax-loss harvesting features available depending on account type
- A relatively low minimum (commonly cited around $100 for many portfolios)

**What to confirm before committing:** country eligibility for Interactive Advisors specifically, separate from standard IBKR brokerage account eligibility - confirm this directly, since automated advisory services sometimes carry additional country-specific restrictions beyond the base brokerage account.

---

## The Alternative: Building Your Own "Robo" Portfolio Manually

For many international investors, the practical alternative to a true robo-advisor is simpler than it sounds: a small number of broad ETFs (covered in our [strategies category](/strategies/)), bought on a regular schedule, rebalanced once or twice a year. This does not offer automatic tax-loss harvesting or algorithmic rebalancing, but it achieves most of what a robo-advisor's core value proposition actually is - diversified, low-cost, hands-off investing - without needing a platform that may not accept your country of residence in the first place.
